# Google Search Console — monthly ritual (≈20 minutes)

Ignore vanity traffic and paid “link packages.” Use this checklist once a month.

## 1. Queries
What you actually appear for (always partly surprising).  
**Action:** New unexpected queries → candidate post ideas (especially ES).

## 2. High impressions, low CTR
Title or meta problem.  
**Action:** Rewrite title tag / meta description; re-request indexing if needed.

## 3. Position 8–20 pages
Closest to breaking through.  
**Action:** One strong internal link from a related post, or a genuine content refresh (`dateModified` + real edit).

## 4. Pages with zero impressions
Indexing or relevance problem.  
**Action:** URL Inspection → confirm indexed; check canonical / hreflang; strengthen on-page match to the query.

## Also glance at
- Sitemap status (`https://www.zaydio.com/sitemap.xml`)
- EN vs ES coverage gaps
- **“This helped” counts** on posts (better strategic signal than raw clicks)

## Skip
- Keyword-rank theater for terms nobody searches
- Tools selling links or guaranteed rankings
- Meta keywords / speakable schema
