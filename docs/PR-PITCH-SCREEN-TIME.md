## Email template

**Subject:** The kids' music company that tells parents videos don't work

Hi [name],

We make music videos for toddlers. Last month we published a post explaining the research on why toddlers learn less from screens than from live interaction — including from our own videos.

It felt strange to publish. It's also the most-read thing on our site.

The short version: the "video deficit" is real and well-replicated, but it's actually a *responsiveness* deficit — in one study, toddlers learned new words over video chat but not from prerecorded video of the same person. Which means co-viewing, singing along, and rewatching close most of the gap, and video calls with grandparents aren't the problem anyone thinks they are.

Happy to share the research, or connect you with Bradley Schachter, who writes and performs all our songs — he's been a working musician for 45 years and started writing for kids when his grandchildren arrived.
