Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Our commitment to families

Zaydio creates kids music and family content. We designed zaydio.com to be simple, safe, and transparent for parents and caregivers.

Information we collect

We do not offer accounts or sign-up forms for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand general site usage (pages visited, button clicks). IP addresses are anonymized. We do not use advertising or remarketing features.

If you choose “Essential only,” analytics are not loaded.

Third-party embeds

When you play music or videos, you may interact with:

  • Spotify and Apple Music embeds
  • YouTube video players
  • Instagram-hosted short video (via our API)

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend parental supervision when children use external platforms.

What we do not do

  • No sale of personal information
  • No third-party advertising on zaydio.com
  • No chat, comments, or user-generated content on our site

Data retention

GA4 data is retained per Google’s default settings (typically 14 months). You can clear your cookie preference by clearing browser storage for zaydio.com.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via Linktree or our social channels linked from the homepage.
