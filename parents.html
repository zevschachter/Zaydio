Questions Parents & Grandparents Ask

What age is Zaydio for?

Zaydio is designed for children ages 2–8, though we’ve heard from plenty of older kids, parents, and grandparents who sing along too. The songs are simple enough for toddlers and engaging enough to keep preschoolers coming back.

Is Zaydio educational?

Yes! Our albums cover letter recognition, phonics, rhythm, movement, and positive social themes like friendship and kindness — all wrapped in music that actually sounds good!

Does Zaydio collect information from children?

No. We don’t have accounts, sign-ups, or forms that collect children’s information. We use basic anonymous analytics to improve the site. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can teachers use this in class?

Absolutely! Zaydio music is perfect for classroom sing-alongs, movement breaks, and circle time! Stream directly from Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube — no account needed!

Where can we stream?

Zaydio is available on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube! Visit our Videos page to watch or click below to start streaming!

How often does Zaydio release new music?

We’re always working on something new! New music videos and albums drop regularly — follow us on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook to be the first to know!

Are there ads on zaydio.com?

None. Zaydio.com is completely ad-free. We believe kids deserve a distraction-free experience.
